# Challenge 04 — New world

**The quest:** Teach tiny-jepa a world that isn't OrbyWorld.

**Your move:** Bring your own environment — a maze, a catching game, a
traffic intersection, anything with frames + actions + rewards. Train with
`train_on_data` or `train_on_env` and show the model hunting / solving /
surviving in it.

**Win condition:** A PR with (1) your env code, (2) a trained checkpoint,
(3) a short write-up: what transferred from the JEPA recipe, what didn't,
and one thing that surprised you. The write-up matters as much as the
score — this challenge is about mapping the territory.

**Stretch:** An env where the *flat* model fails but your architectural
tweak succeeds. That's a finding, not just a demo.
